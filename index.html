⚠️Weight-gain warning: don't turn this into a hard interval session — it burns the calories you're trying to keep. Easy pace only, and eat a real meal afterwards.
📈Weight gain basicsEat ~300–500 kcal above maintenance. 1.6–2 g protein per kg bodyweight. Weigh yourself once a week, same morning. Target 0.25–0.5 kg per week — faster is mostly fat.
